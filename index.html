Digital SAT: Information & Ideas Mastery for International Students

1. Structure of "Information and Ideas"

The Digital SAT Reading and Writing section dedicates approximately 26% (12–14 questions per 54-item test) to the Information and Ideas domain. This module contains 28 high-level items spanning:

  • Central Ideas and Details: Extract the primary thesis without being trapped by hyper-specific, partially true details.
  • Command of Evidence (Textual & Quantitative): Discern which qualitative citation or data trend directly validates, illustrates, or falsifies an academic claim.
  • Inferences: Deduce the single, strictly unavoidable logical conclusion that completes an argument without extending past the passage's boundary.

2. Crucial Distractor Archetypes to Eliminate

A. The Extreme Leap

Uses definitive qualifiers like solely, entirely, impossible, always when the stimulus uses probabilistic language (frequently, tend to, often).

B. The Faulty Reversal

Inverts cause-and-effect or conditional relationships (e.g., claiming high costs caused nearshoring rather than risk-mitigation justifying higher costs).

C. True in Reality, Unsupported in Text

Relies on real-world background knowledge of economics or politics that is neither mentioned nor implied in the passage.

D. Quantitative Misdirection

Cites accurate isolated numbers from the table but misrepresents the comparative trend or answers a different analytical question.

3. Key Macroeconomic & Academic Glossary

Nearshoring / Friendshoring
Transferring business operations to nearby countries or diplomatically aligned nations rather than the lowest-cost distant hub.
Carbon Leakage & CBAM
When companies transfer production to countries with laxer emission standards, counteracted by carbon border taxes proportional to embedded carbon intensity.
Dutch Disease
When a natural resource export boom causes currency appreciation, making non-resource manufacturing and agriculture uncompetitive.
Parametric Insurance
Insurance contracts that automatically issue payouts based on objective index triggers (e.g. satellite rainfall data) without requiring manual on-site damage inspection.
Last-Mile Liquidity Bottleneck
The localized financial friction and conversion spreads incurred when digital funds are converted into local cash or bank deposits in recipient countries.
